Browser Text to Speech Reader

Use a browser text to speech reader to turn notes, study material, and digital documents into spoken audio. It keeps your reading workflow simple, private, and focused in one place.

Try the tool

Why people use it

  • Listen to notes and revision content without staring at a screen for long periods.
  • Improve focus by pairing reading with audio and repetition.
  • Use it in the browser for quick study sessions without uploading content to a server.

How it works

  1. Paste text, or upload a TXT, PDF, or DOCX.
  2. Adjust group size, speed, and language in the reader.
  3. Listen while you review, revise, and learn.

Frequently asked questions

What is a browser text to speech reader?

A browser text to speech reader converts written text into spoken audio inside the web browser. It is ideal for learning, revision, and reading long notes with less eye strain.

Can I use it with study notes?

Yes. It is designed for notes, summaries, essays, and reading material that is easier to absorb by ear.

Does it keep my content private?

The main workflow is local-first and browser-based, helping keep your notes on the device rather than in a remote application server.
