Read PDF Aloud Online

Reading a PDF aloud online helps you review dense material without spending hours staring at the screen. DICTATE turns document text into spoken audio for more comfortable revision and study sessions.

Try the tool

Who this helps

  • Students reviewing academic documents and lecture notes.
  • Researchers and professionals listening to long reading material.
  • Anyone who wants a quieter, more focused study workflow.

How it works

  1. Open DICTATE and upload your PDF or DOCX file.
  2. Choose your speech pace, grouping, and language.
  3. Listen while you review the material in manageable chunks.

Frequently asked questions

Can I read a PDF aloud online without software?

Yes. The browser-based workflow is built for direct use without installing a separate app.

Does it support study documents?

Yes. It is useful for research papers, reading summaries, and revision notes that are easier to absorb by ear.

Is it private?

DICTATE keeps the process browser-first and local-first so the reading experience can stay focused on your device.
