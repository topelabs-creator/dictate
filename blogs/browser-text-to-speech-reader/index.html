Browser Text to Speech Reader

Use a browser text to speech reader to turn notes, study material, and digital documents into spoken audio. It keeps your reading workflow simple, private, and focused in one place.

Why people use it

  • Listen to notes and revision content without staring at a screen for long periods.
  • Improve focus by pairing reading with audio and repetition.
  • Use it in the browser for quick study sessions without uploading content to a server.

How it works

  1. Paste text, or upload a TXT, PDF, or DOCX.
  2. Adjust group size, speed, and language in the reader.
  3. Listen while you review, revise, and learn.
