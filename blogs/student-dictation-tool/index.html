Student Dictation Tool

A student-friendly dictation and listening workflow for essays, revision notes, and study material that helps you absorb information more effectively.

Why people use it

  • Create a listening session from course notes or article summaries.
  • Use smaller reading groups for calmer revision.
  • Switch languages and pace to match your study routine.

How it works

  1. Paste or upload a study note.
  2. Choose the reading rhythm and repetition settings.
  3. Listen and review key ideas in shorter, clearer chunks.
